# The career ladder — what comes after senior

## Two paths

After a few years, the path branches:

- **Engineering** — staff, principal, architect: influence through system design,
  technical decisions, and what you teach others.
- **Management** — influence through people, priorities, and building a team.

These are not higher and lower positions. Becoming a manager is **changing
professions**, not a promotion. You can also move back.

## Neighboring roles

In a large company, similar ladders exist for other specialties:

- **SRE** — reliability, automation, and on-call work.
- **Network architect** — network design at regional scale.
- **[[qa-sdet|SDET]]** — an engineer focused on system testability, with the same salary bands as SWE.

## What moves you up

Not years of experience or lines of code. Rather, **the scope of the problems you
take responsibility for**. A junior solves a task; a senior solves a problem;
a staff engineer helps stop that problem recurring across the team.
